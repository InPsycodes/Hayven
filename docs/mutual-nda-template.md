# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement — Hayven — TEMPLATE

> **Template, not a document ready to sign.** Unlike the Therapist Agreement or DPIA,
> an NDA is genuinely standard boilerplate with little business judgment involved — this
> is close to ready to use as-is, but still needs a lawyer's once-over before it's used
> for real (NDAs are simple in structure but a badly-worded one is still unenforceable),
> and the bracketed fields below filled in per use.

**Use for:** any conversation with a potential investor, partner, contractor, or vendor
where confidential information needs to be shared before a formal engagement exists —
before a Therapist Agreement, Employment Agreement, or Vendor Agreement is signed, not
instead of one.

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore [DESCRIBE THE PURPOSE — e.g. "a potential business
partnership," "a potential investment," "a potential clinical advisory engagement"] (the
"Purpose"), and in doing so may disclose confidential information to each other. This
Agreement governs how that information is protected.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party (the
"Disclosing Party") to the other (the "Receiving Party"), whether in writing, orally, or
by any other means, that is either marked or described as confidential at the time of
disclosure, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its
nature and the circumstances of disclosure. This includes, without limitation: business
plans, financial information, technical and product information, user and clinical data
handling practices, therapist and family information, and the terms of this Agreement
and any discussions under it.

**Confidential Information does not include** information that: (a) was already known to
the Receiving Party without an obligation of confidentiality before disclosure; (b) is
or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (c) is
independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to the Disclosing
Party's Confidential Information; or (d) is rightfully received from a third party
without a duty of confidentiality.

**For the avoidance of doubt:** actual data about Hayven's families, children, or
therapists — clinical records, session notes, personal contact information — is never an
appropriate subject of disclosure under this Agreement or for the Purpose described in
Section 1, regardless of how "Confidential Information" is defined above. This Agreement
governs business and technical confidentiality between the Parties; it is not a
mechanism for sharing real user data with a third party, and nothing in this Agreement
should be read as authorising that.

## 3. Obligations

Each Party, as a Receiving Party, agrees to:

- Use the other Party's Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
- Protect it with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential
  information of similar importance, and in any case no less than reasonable care;
- Not disclose it to any third party without the Disclosing Party's prior written
  consent, except to its own employees, officers, or professional advisers who need to
  know it for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as
  protective as this Agreement;
- Not use it to compete with, or to develop a competing product or service to, the
  Disclosing Party.

## 4. Required disclosure

If a Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, or a valid court or regulatory
order to disclose Confidential Information, it may do so, but will (where legally
permitted) give the Disclosing Party prompt notice before disclosing, so the Disclosing
Party has an opportunity to seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy.

## 5. No licence, no obligation to proceed

Nothing in this Agreement grants either Party any licence or right to the other's
Confidential Information beyond what's needed for the Purpose, and nothing in this
Agreement obligates either Party to enter into any further agreement, transaction, or
relationship.

## 6. Term

This Agreement's confidentiality obligations begin on the date above and continue for
[TERM — e.g. "three (3) years from the date of disclosure of the relevant Confidential
Information," a common default; counsel may prefer "indefinitely" for genuinely
sensitive categories]. Either Party may end the Parties' discussions under the Purpose
at any time by written notice; the confidentiality obligations survive that.

## 7. Return or destruction

On the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party will promptly return or
destroy all materials containing the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in its
possession, and confirm this in writing — except for one archival copy retained solely
to comply with legal or audit obligations, which remains subject to this Agreement.

## 8. Remedies

Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for
which monetary damages alone would be an inadequate remedy, and that the non-breaching
Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy available at
law or in equity.

## 9. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Any dispute
arising from it is subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Lagos State.

## 10. General

This Agreement is the entire understanding between the Parties regarding its subject
matter and supersedes any prior discussions on the same subject. It may only be amended
in writing signed by both Parties. If any provision is found unenforceable, the rest of
the Agreement remains in effect.

## Notes for whoever uses this template

- This is **mutual** (both sides protect each other's information) — appropriate for
  investor/partner/advisor conversations where Hayven might also learn something
  confidential about the other side. If a situation is genuinely one-directional (only
  Hayven is disclosing), a one-way NDA is simpler and shouldn't grant the counterparty
  reciprocal obligations they have no actual information to protect.
- Section 2's carve-out (real user/clinical data is never in scope) exists because a
  generic NDA template, used carelessly, could otherwise read as authorising sharing
  actual family or child data with an outside party "for the Purpose" — that's never
  what this document is for, and the clause makes that explicit rather than relying on
  good judgment alone.
- Have a lawyer confirm the term length in Section 6 and the governing-law/jurisdiction
  clause in Section 9 match what Hayven's other documents (Terms of Service, Therapist
  Agreement) already commit to, so nothing conflicts across documents.
