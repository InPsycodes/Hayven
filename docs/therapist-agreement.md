## Why this document exists

Right now, the only thing a therapist agrees to is Hayven's general Terms of Service —
the same document every parent also clicks through. It asserts therapists are
"independent practitioners... not Hayven employees" and that Hayven "does not warrant
the ongoing conduct, availability, or clinical outcomes of any therapist" — but a
classification and a liability disclaimer sitting inside a platform-wide consumer terms
document is a thin foundation for what's actually at stake: a therapist is making real
clinical judgments about a child, getting paid through an automated payout system, and
handling some of the most sensitive data the platform holds. This agreement is meant to
be the thing that actually governs that relationship, signed specifically by the
therapist, before they can be assigned a child.

## 2. What the Platform provides, and what you provide

**The Platform provides:** a public profile once verified, a booking and scheduling
system, secure payment collection and payout processing, a structured system for
recording session notes and publishing reports to parents, messaging with the families
you work with, and administrative support (verification, disputes, technical support).

**You provide:** the actual therapy — your own clinical judgment, your own professional
standards, and your own conduct with each child and family, consistent with your
licensing body's code of practice and the standards in Section 6 below.

## 3. Verification and ongoing eligibility

Before your profile is listed publicly, you submit credentials, licensing information,
and supporting documents for review. This is a genuine check, not a formality — but
passing it once doesn't mean it's permanent:

- You must keep your licence and any credentials you listed current, and tell us
  promptly if any lapse, are suspended, or are revoked.
- A verified profile requires, in addition to credential approval: at least one open
  availability slot, and a bank account on file for payouts — you won't appear in
  search without both, even once verified (this is enforced by the Platform's
  discovery system, not a manual check).
- We can suspend or revoke your verified status, or your access to a specific child's
  case, if a concern is raised under the safeguarding procedure, if you're the subject
  of an active investigation, or if you breach this agreement or the Platform's
  Acceptable Use terms.

## 4. Fees, commission, and payment

This section states the Platform's actual current commission structure, as
implemented — not a proposal.

- **Commission**: the Platform retains **20%** of each session's charge; you receive
  **80%** (net of that commission).
- **Pricing**: you set your own price per session, shown to parents before they book.
- **Earnings clear 8 days** after a session (the 7-day window in which a parent can
  report a problem, plus one day), then pay out **weekly, every Monday**,
  once your cleared balance is at or above **₦5,000** (balances under that threshold
  roll over to the next week).
- **A bank account on file is required** before any payout can be made; you're
  responsible for keeping your bank details current and accurate.
- **Cancellations and no-shows**: governed by the Platform's cancellation policy (see
  the Platform's Terms of Service, and the specific retained-earning rules that apply
  when a session is cancelled late or a family doesn't attend) — you receive your
  commission-split share of any amount the Platform retains under that policy.
- **Disputes and refunds**: if a booking is refunded (through the dispute process or
  otherwise), any earning already recorded against that booking is reversed before
  payout, or clawed back from a future payout if it was already paid.
- Commission and payout terms may change with **at least 30 days' written notice**
  to existing therapists (decided 2026-10-09). A change never applies to a session
  booked before the change takes effect.

## 5. Confidentiality and data protection

- You will treat everything you learn about a child and their family through the
  Platform as confidential, and access it only for the purpose of providing care to
  children actually assigned to you.
- You will not export, copy, or store a child's clinical data outside the Platform
  except as necessary for your own professional record-keeping obligations under your
  licensing body's rules, and even then, only in a manner that keeps it secure.
- You will not attempt to access, or ask anyone else to access, a child's record you
  are not assigned to.
- The confidential-notes field exists specifically so you can record clinical
  observations not appropriate for a parent-facing report; use it as intended, and
  understand that a supervisor assigned to you may also see it.
- You acknowledge the Platform's Privacy Policy and this agreement together govern how
  you may use data accessed through the Platform, and that the Platform may audit
  access to clinical records for safety and compliance purposes.

## 6. Professional standards and conduct

- You will provide services consistent with your professional licensing body's code of
  ethics and practice standards, and applicable Nigerian law.
- You will not represent yourself as a Hayven employee, or represent the Platform as
  providing clinical supervision of your practice (a Platform-side "supervisor" role,
  where it applies, is an oversight relationship for platform purposes, not a
  substitute for your own required clinical supervision under your licensing body).
- You will not attempt to arrange or accept payment for a session outside the Platform
  once a family found you through Hayven, for a period of **12 months** after your last
  Platform-facilitated session with that family (decided 2026-10-09; counsel to confirm
  it's enforceable as drafted, not as a restraint of trade).
- You accept the Platform's safeguarding procedure (`docs/safeguarding-procedure.md`,
  once finalised) as a condition of practising on Hayven, including the duty to raise a
  concern about a child's safety through the defined channel rather than handling it
  independently.

## 8. Term, suspension, and termination

- This agreement begins when you accept it and ends when your account is closed, by
  either party.
- **You may stop practising on the Platform at any time** by requesting account
  closure; outstanding cleared earnings are still paid out on the normal schedule.
- **The Platform may suspend your access immediately, without prior notice**, if a
  safeguarding concern is raised about you, if you're under investigation for a
  credential or conduct issue, or if immediate suspension is necessary to protect a
  child or the integrity of the Platform. You'll be told the reason as soon as it's
  safe and appropriate to do so.
- **The Platform may otherwise terminate this agreement with 30 days' written
  notice**, for a material breach of this agreement, the
  Terms of Service, or the Acceptable Use policy, or for any other reason with that
  same notice.
- On termination, any cleared, unpaid earnings are still paid out on the normal
  schedule; the Platform does not owe you future bookings or a minimum volume of work
  at any time.
